# Selling B2B SaaS to Customers

## Understanding Your Customer

1. **Identify Decision Makers**: Know who makes the buying decisions at your target companies
2. **Understand Pain Points**: Learn the specific problems your prospects face
3. **Map the Buying Process**: Understand how decisions are made in your target organizations

## Sales Strategy

### Lead Generation

- **Content Marketing**: Whitepapers, case studies, and blog posts
- **Webinars and Events**: Educational webinars on relevant topics
- **Outbound Sales**: Targeted email campaigns and cold outreach
- **Referrals**: Incentivize existing customers to refer new clients

### Sales Process

1. **Discovery**: Understand prospect needs and pain points
2. **Demo**: Show how your product addresses their specific challenges
3. **Proposal**: Provide clear pricing and implementation timeline
4. **Negotiation**: Address objections and customize offering
5. **Close**: Finalize agreement and prepare for onboarding

### Customer Success

- **Onboarding**: Structured process to get customers started
- **Training**: Comprehensive training materials
- **Support**: Quick response to issues
- **Account Management**: Regular check-ins and strategy discussions

## Pricing Models

- **Per User/Seat**: Charge based on number of users
- **Tiered Plans**: Basic, Professional, Enterprise with different features
- **Usage-Based**: Pay for what you use (API calls, storage, etc.)
- **Value-Based**: Price based on value delivered
- **Annual vs. Monthly**: Incentivize annual commitments

## Demonstrating ROI

- Calculate and communicate clear ROI metrics
- Use case studies from similar customers
- Offer proof of concept or trial period
